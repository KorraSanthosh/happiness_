# Demo data attribution

Derived (resampled, harmonised) from datasets licensed CC BY 4.0:

- Shanghai T2DM (Zhao et al. 2023, CC BY 4.0) - Type 2, with clinical EHR
- Shanghai T1DM (Zhao et al. 2023, CC BY 4.0) - Type 1, with clinical EHR
- AZT1D (Mayo Clinic Arizona 2025, CC BY 4.0) - Type 1
- T1D-UOM (Univ. of Manchester 2025, CC BY 4.0) - Type 1
- HUPA-UCM (Hidalgo et al. 2024, CC BY 4.0) - Type 1

Shanghai: Zhao et al., *Chinese diabetes datasets for data-driven machine learning*, Sci Data 2023, doi:10.6084/m9.figshare.20444397.
HUPA-UCM: Hidalgo et al., Data in Brief 2024, doi:10.17632/3hbcscwz44.1.
T1D-UOM: University of Manchester, doi:10.5281/zenodo.15806142.
AZT1D: Mayo Clinic Arizona, doi:10.17632/gk9m674wcx.1.
